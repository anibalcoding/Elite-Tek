Owner & Founder

Nico Zaldana

Nico Zaldana is the Owner and Founder of Elite Tek Soccer Academy, with a playing and club background that includes experience in youth environments and clubs across Europe. His understanding of the game, combined with years of individual player development, has made Elite Tek a trusted training destination for players at every stage of the pathway.

Nico works with ambitious youth and academy players as well as collegiate and professional athletes who seek him out for specialized training and continued development. His approach is built on technical excellence, game intelligence, attention to detail, and preparing players to meet the demands of the highest levels of the game.

Owner & Co-Founder

Ricky Mejia

Ricky Mejia is the Co-Founder of EliteTek Soccer Academy and has dedicated his life to the game as both a player and coach. His passion for player development is reflected in his attention to detail, high-energy coaching style, and game-realistic training sessions.
