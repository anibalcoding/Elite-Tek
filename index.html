Owner

Nico Zaldana

Nico Zaldana is the Founder and Head Coach of Elite Tek Soccer Academy. A product of elite academies across Europe and the Americas, Nico brings a global perspective to player development that few coaches in the Dallas area can match. His philosophy is built on the technical precision and tactical intelligence he absorbed training at some of the world's most respected programs.

Under his guidance, players have improved their first touch, sharpened their decision-making, and earned spots on higher-level club teams. At Elite Tek, he builds more than skill. He builds the complete player.

Ricky Mejia
